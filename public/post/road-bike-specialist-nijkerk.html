Road bike specialist in Nijkerk: where your bike is in good hands

If you own a road bike in or around Nijkerk, you want a workshop that understands what you ask of your bike. This is what to expect from a road bike specialist who races himself and has worked in UCI teams.

A road bike is not an ordinary bike. A gear that does not quite shift in one position, a saddle a fraction too high or a cable that does not run freely: you feel it on every ride, and in a race it can make the difference. That is why many cyclists in Nijkerk and the surrounding area look not just for a bike shop, but for a road bike specialist.

What makes a road bike specialist different?

A good road bike specialist does not only look at what is broken, but at how the bike works as a whole: drivetrain, brakes, wheels, position and the way you ride. The difference lies in experience with exactly this kind of bike and in the will to keep going until it is right.

For those who train or race seriously

If you ride kilometres every week, ride with a club or start races, you want your bike to do the same thing every time. You do not want to find out in the middle of a descent that something is wrong, and you want a workshop that understands you have a date for a race.

At S40 Fietsservice, Singel 40, that is daily practice. The cyclists behind the workbench have more than twenty years of experience in Europe, from amateur to professional, worked as mechanics in UCI teams and prepare bikes to UCI rules. They do not stop when the gears shift, but when they work perfectly in every position. And because they ride themselves, they understand why your bike has to be ready on time.

A problem nobody solves?

Sometimes a bike has a problem that several workshops fail to solve. S40 Fietsservice takes on such jobs. The time the team spends investigating the problem and looking for the best solution is not charged. In a Google review, a customer writes that other shops could not fix it and that at S40 it was repaired straight away and fast.

What can you have done?

You can go to S40 Fietsservice for maintenance and repair, race preparation, parts and accessories, and a custom bike, new or second-hand. Set-up and bike fit are part of it too: read what a bike fit is. Besides road bikes, the team works on gravel bikes, track bikes, BMX bikes and triathlon bikes. For gravel bike maintenance, read on here. The usual turnaround is 24 hours.
