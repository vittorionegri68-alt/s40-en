Statistics without cookies

We measure the use of this website with Google Analytics 4 in a cookieless configuration: no cookies are set, no information is stored in your browser and IP addresses are anonymised. We do not build profiles of visitors and do not share data for advertising. That is why no cookie banner is needed.

Requesting an appointment

The form on the website does not send any data itself. It opens the email app on your device with a ready-made message to us. What you fill in, you send yourself by email. We only use that data to answer your request and plan the appointment.

Map and weather

  • The Google Maps map only loads after you click “Show map”. From that moment Google’s terms and privacy policy apply.
  • The weather data comes from MET Norway. It is fetched by our own server; your data is not sent to MET Norway.
  • The route maps are drawn by us from GPX files. No third-party map services are loaded.

Hosting and links to other sites

The website is hosted on Vercel. As with any web server, technical data such as the IP address may be processed briefly to deliver and secure the site. Links to Instagram, LinkedIn and Google take you to websites of those parties, with their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to object to processing. Send an email to service@s40.nl. Do you have a complaint? You can submit it to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).
